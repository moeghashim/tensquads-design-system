How it works

Set up in an afternoon, not a quarter

  1. 1

    Tell us about the business

    New or existing, what you sell, where you sell it. A couple of sentences is enough to brief the squad.

  2. 2

    Pick your agents and connect

    Choose the agents you want and connect your store, inbox and ads. Each agent gets its own isolated machine and only the keys it needs.

  3. 3

    Set the limits, then let go

    Decide what agents can do alone and what needs a yes from you. Loosen the limits as you build trust.

You stay in control

Agents work inside limits you set. Anything bigger, they ask.

  • Spend and refund caps. Per agent, per day. Anything over the cap lands in your approval inbox with the reasoning.
  • Every action is logged. See who asked what, which skill ran and what changed, message by message.
  • Isolated by default. Each agent runs on its own machine with its own secrets and an allowlist of sites it may reach.
  • Pause anything, any time. One switch per agent, one per skill. Paused agents keep their memory and pick up where they left off.
